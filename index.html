App Description

Virus VS Vaccine is a Unity-based strategy simulation in which a player manages a spreading virus across a network of connected locations by allocating a limited weekly budget of Efficiency Points toward vaccination and shutdown interventions. The project responds to the ongoing real-world need for tools that make networked, resource-constrained public health trade-offs tangible rather than abstract. We describe the three linked systems that make this possible: a location-and-connection network, a shutdown intervention state machine, and an Efficiency Point economy, and walk through a representative code sample from each. An experiment comparing network-severing interventions against vaccination found vaccination consistently more effective at limiting total infections, a result consistent with published findings on the limited effectiveness of travel restrictions alone. We compare our approach against three existing methodologies, three compartmental modeling, cellular-automata simulation, and branching-scenario games, and outline the calibration and state-management improvements that further development would prioritize.
